# Comprehensive Checklist of Perimenopause Symptoms

Perimenopause is the transitional phase leading up to menopause, during which fluctuating estrogen and progesterone levels affect various physiological and psychological systems. Because hormone receptors exist throughout the body, symptoms extend far beyond menstrual changes.

This document presents an expanded list of **60 perimenopause symptoms**, structured into clear system-based categories with direct **Yes / No** diagnostic assessment questions for clinical or health-app intake.

## 12. Clinical Notes & Verification Sources

### Medical Framework
- **STRAW+10 (Stages of Reproductive Aging Workshop)**: Global gold standard defining reproductive aging stages, placing bleeding pattern changes as the key diagnostic marker.
- **The Menopause Society (formerly NAMS)**: Recognizes vasomotor symptoms, sleep disruption, mood changes, and genitourinary symptoms as primary hallmarks.
- **SWAN (Study of Women's Health Across the Nation)**: Multi-center longitudinal study confirming systemic midlife symptoms including musculoskeletal aches, brain fog, and psychological symptoms.
- **British Menopause Society (BMS) & NHS UK**: Acknowledge secondary neuro-sensory and mucosal manifestations (formication, paresthesia, burning mouth syndrome, tinnitus).
